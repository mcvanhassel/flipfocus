# Privacy Policy - Iteratus

*Last updated: October 20, 2026*

Iteratus is a recurring-tasks planner for life maintenance: the home, the car,
pets, paperwork and everything else that comes back on a schedule. It is
designed to be **local-first**: your data belongs on your device and stays
there.

## What data the app uses

**Tasks, history and settings.** The tasks you create, their groups, due
dates, completion history, notes, costs and your app settings are stored
**only on your device**.

- This data is **never uploaded, transmitted, shared, or sold**. The app has
  no server and no user accounts.
- Deleting the app erases it. You can also remove individual tasks, assets or
  history entries inside the app at any time.

**Assets and photos (Pro).** Asset cards can hold details such as notes,
costs, links and photos.

- If you add a photo, the app asks for access to your camera or photo library
  at that moment. It uses this access only to let you take or pick the photo
  you want to attach.
- Photos are stored in the app's own storage on your device. They are not
  uploaded anywhere.
- You can revoke camera and photo access at any time in your system settings.
  Existing photos in the app are not affected.
- A link you add to an asset card opens in your system browser only when you
  tap it. Iteratus does not visit it for you.

**Notifications.** Reminders and the optional daily summary are **local
notifications** scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent from a server, no
push tokens are created, and you can switch each kind off in Settings or in
your system settings.

**Widgets and app badge.** Home-screen and lock-screen widgets and the app
icon badge show your task counts. The data they display is shared only
between the app and its widget on your device.

**Purchases.** The one-time Pro unlock is processed by Apple's App Store or
Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. Purchase status is
checked with the store and can be restored through it ("Restore purchases").
This is the only network activity of the app.

**Location.** The app does **not** request or use your location.

## What the app does NOT do

- No analytics, tracking, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
- No user accounts, no cloud sync, no server operated by the developer.
- No sharing or selling of any personal data, ever.
- No data collection from children or anyone else.

## Backup, export and import

Backup, CSV and calendar (ICS) files are generated on your device. They leave
your device only when you explicitly save or share them via your system share
sheet. Iteratus does not upload them. Importing a file reads it on your
device only.

Your phone's own backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) may include the
app's data, depending on your device settings. That is controlled by Apple or
Google and your device settings, not by Iteratus.

## Languages and templates

Iteratus uses your device language, or the language you pick in Settings. The
built-in template packs are bundled with the app. Using them does not send any
data anywhere.

## Children

Iteratus is a general-purpose planner and is not directed at children. The
app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because all of your data stays on your device and the developer receives none
of it, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export or delete
on your behalf. You are in full control: you can edit or remove your data in
the app, export it from Settings, or erase everything by deleting the app.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that change how data is handled (for
example optional sync between devices), this policy will be updated first and
the app will ask for your consent before any new data use. The date at the top
shows when this policy last changed.
